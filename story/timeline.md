# Story Timeline

The controlled timeline is maintained in [Master Timeline](../docs/03-master-timeline.md). This file is a short navigational summary.

- **Before 18 Oct 1998:** Mayor Thomas Wren finds discrepancies in land-project documents and opposes the project. Evelyn has already begun examining the land records and correspondence irregularities.
- **18 Oct 1998:** A warehouse fire near the Riverside Industrial Zone late Sunday evening kills Thomas. This date is inferred from Newspaper 01, dated Tuesday, 20 Oct 1998.
- **20 Oct 1998:** The Bellwether Chronicle publishes Newspaper 01, reporting Thomas's death and the disputed Riverside development.
- **October 1998:** Evelyn's inquiry continues, now potentially including the circumstances of her father's death. She disappears later in the month; her fate remains unknown. Letter dates, last confirmed contact, and unverified sightings must be kept distinct.
- **21 Oct 2023, 8:00 PM:** A fire damages an unused storage building near Riverside, as reported in Newspaper 02.
- **23 Oct 2023:** The Bellwether Chronicle publishes Newspaper 02, reporting the second fire. No connection to the 1998 fire is established.
- **October 2026:** A third fire near Riverside prompts renewed inquiry. Newspaper 03 does not specify an exact event or publication date. A box of undelivered letters is found during archival handling, and the player begins the investigation.

The dates printed or explicitly reported in the newspaper assets control unless the assets are deliberately revised. The three fires are not proven to share a cause or perpetrator.
