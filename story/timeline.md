# Story Timeline

The controlled timeline is maintained in [Master Timeline](../docs/03-master-timeline.md). This file is a short navigational summary.

- **Before/within 1998:** Mayor Thomas Wren finds discrepancies in land-project documents and opposes the project.
- **1998:** The first fire kills Thomas. Evelyn, age 19 and a first-year journalism student, investigates the records and disappears in October.
- **2023:** A second fire occurs in a nearby area. The 1998 file is reviewed again.
- **2026:** A third nearby fire prompts renewed inquiry. A box of undelivered letters is found during archival handling, and the player begins the investigation.

Exact fire dates and some document dates remain open continuity items. Do not invent dates; use TBD until reconciled in the master timeline.
