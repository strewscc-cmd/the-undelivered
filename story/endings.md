# Ending Boundaries

## Canonical ending position

The final report is an evidence-based interpretation, not a definitive confession or full solution.

The strongest theory is that deliberate burning may explain the pattern, but the evidence does not conclusively prove arson or identify one perpetrator. Ashbourne benefited from changes in land status/use, but its involvement in any fire remains unproven. Evelyn may have been targeted to silence her investigation, yet her ultimate fate remains unknown.

## What the ending must not do

- Reveal an unsupported mastermind.
- Confirm that Thomas was murdered without definitive evidence.
- Declare Ashbourne guilty based solely on financial benefit.
- Confirm Evelyn's death or escape without proof.
- Treat every irregular record as forged.
- Turn a suspicious pattern into certainty.

## What the ending should achieve

- Show the player's best-supported explanation.
- Make the player distinguish facts from inferences.
- Acknowledge at least one important unresolved question.
- Reward careful reading, cross-referencing, and calibrated language.
