# Progressive Hints

## Hint policy

Hints should support student reasoning and reduce frustration without revealing a conclusion that has not been earned. Hint use may affect the investigation independence score, not the English Proficiency Score.

## Q01 — What Is She Really Saying?
1. **Observation:** Look for language about her plans and how other people seem to know them.
2. **Connection:** Compare Evelyn's tone here with the ordinary friendship context in F10.
3. **Reasoning:** Choose passages that show fear or leaked information, but do not infer physical surveillance unless the text says so.

## Q02 — A Threat in Polite Words
1. **Observation:** Find the sentence about Evelyn's future or reputation.
2. **Connection:** Compare it with the institutional concern expressed in F09.
3. **Reasoning:** Explain how a polite formal phrase can imply pressure without being an explicit threat of violence.

## Q03 — The Date That Doesn't Match
1. **Observation:** Write down each date attached to F03 and F07.
2. **Connection:** Match each exact reference number against D01 and E13.
3. **Reasoning:** Separate the date a letter was written from the date it was processed.

## Q04 — Who Does “They” Mean?
1. **Observation:** Reread the full sentence and the paragraph around the pronoun.
2. **Connection:** Compare the context with F05.
3. **Reasoning:** Choose only the referent supported by context; proximity alone is not enough.

## Q05 — Restore the Missing Paragraph
1. **Observation:** Read the sentence immediately before and after the gap.
2. **Connection:** Check the difference between original and official records in D02.
3. **Reasoning:** Select the paragraph that is coherent and supported, not the most dramatic one.

## Q06 — Three Fires, One Timeline
1. **Observation:** List the year and event described by each source.
2. **Connection:** Compare the locations and the reason the 1998 file was reopened.
3. **Reasoning:** Similar locations and recurring company references support investigation but do not prove a common perpetrator.

## Q07 — The Evidence Board
1. **Observation:** Underline the exact fact stated in each source.
2. **Connection:** Look for independent documents that corroborate it.
3. **Reasoning:** A fact is directly documented; an inference is a reasoned explanation; an unresolved question cannot yet be answered.

## L-07 — Locker 07
1. **Observation:** Read the direction clue carefully and identify which entry/date it refers to.
2. **Connection:** Follow the indicated route on the map.
3. **Reasoning:** Use the rule explicitly established in the clue to calculate the code. The final production version must not rely on arbitrary guessing.

## Q08 — The Final Report
1. **Observation:** Choose at least three pieces of relevant evidence.
2. **Connection:** Sort each one into fact, inference, or unresolved question.
3. **Reasoning:** State the most plausible theory with qualified language, then mention what the evidence cannot prove.
