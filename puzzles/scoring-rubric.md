# Scoring Rubric

## Investigation Score — 100%

- Puzzle accuracy: 40%
- Evidence connections: 35%
- Independent investigation / hint use: 25%

## English Proficiency Score — 100%

- Textual evidence: 30%
- Inference and reasoning: 30%
- Coherence and organization: 20%
- Language clarity: 20%

## Final Report rubric

| Criterion | Points | Highest-level performance |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence use | 30 | Uses at least three relevant sources and explains their connection to the claim. |
| Inference and reasoning | 30 | Distinguishes facts from inferences, notes limitations, and avoids overclaiming. |
| Coherence and organization | 20 | Has a clear claim, logical sequencing, and cohesive links. |
| Language clarity | 20 | Clear B2-appropriate language with controlled grammar and useful hedging. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

Alternative conclusions may receive full credit if supported by evidence and careful reasoning. A theory should not be penalized solely because it differs from the teacher's preferred interpretation.

Hint use affects only the independence component of Investigation Score, not English Proficiency Score.
