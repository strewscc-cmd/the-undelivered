## Voice recording checklist

For every script, prepare:
- exact words
- speaker and context
- emotional intention
- pacing and pauses
- pronunciation notes for names/places
- recommended file name
- transcript/captions
- where the recording appears in the game
- what factual claims the speaker may and may not make

## Newspaper assets

At least three fictional newspaper artifacts are likely useful:

1. **1998 — “Mayor Thomas Wren Dies in Bellwether Fire”**: an early report; should distinguish confirmed death from unconfirmed cause.
2. **2023 — second nearby fire**: reports the fire and explains why the earlier file is being reviewed, without declaring a conspiracy.
3. **2026 — third nearby fire**: triggers the present-day investigation.

Potential supplemental clippings:
- a land-development proposal featuring Ashbourne
- a notice about changes to local land status
- a short follow-up article on the 1998 case

Each clipping needs a publication name, publication date, headline, byline (if any), body text, and visual design notes. The name of the fictional publication is TBD.

## Copyright and production notes

Prefer custom-made fictional artifacts or properly licensed assets. Avoid presenting authentic historical news images as if they were evidence from Bellwether. Maintain a source/credit log for any external visual or sound asset.
