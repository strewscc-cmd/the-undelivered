# 03 — Master Timeline

## Timeline rules

- Distinguish the date a letter was written, mailed, processed, postmarked, received, and discovered.
- A processing date does not prove where the writer was on that date.
- Preserve unknown dates as unknown; do not invent specificity merely to fill a timeline.
- The three fires occurred in nearby areas, not necessarily on the exact same parcel.

## Cross-document rules

1. Never use a 2026 discovery date as though it were the document's original date.
2. Do not give post-1998 knowledge to 1998 characters unless a source justifies it.
3. Do not let a character in 1998 predict the 2023 or 2026 fires.
4. Avoid claiming that all fires have one perpetrator.
5. The company's appearance in records across different dates is a lead, not a conviction.
