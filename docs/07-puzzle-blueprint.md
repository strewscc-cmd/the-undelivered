# 07 — Puzzle Blueprint and Answer Key

## Puzzle principles

- Each puzzle has a complete, fair solution from clues available to the player.
- A puzzle may leave the larger mystery unresolved, but the immediate task must be solvable.
- Use progressive hints: observation, connection, reasoning.
- Separate English correctness from investigation reasoning where feasible.
- Do not treat inferences as confirmed facts.

## Q01 — What Is She Really Saying?

**Sources:** F01, F10  
**Task:** Select two passages suggesting Evelyn feels her plans are known and she has become cautious.  
**Answer logic:** Correct choices must quote the final wording of F01 and be supported by context.  
**English objective:** inference, tone, implied meaning.  
**Limit:** Evelyn's fear does not prove someone physically followed her.

## Q02 — A Threat in Polite Words

**Sources:** F02, F09  
**Task:** Identify language implying consequences if Evelyn continues the investigation.  
**Answer logic:** Select the formal phrase that indirectly warns about harm to her future/reputation. A phrase such as “an otherwise promising future could be compromised” may be used in the final letter text.  
**English objective:** formal register and implied threat.  
**Limit:** pressure does not prove murder or arson.

## Q03 — The Date That Doesn't Match

**Sources:** F03, F07, D01, E13  
**Task:** Match letter references against written, processing, and ledger dates.  
**Answer logic:** Evidence supports irregular processing/retention of specified letters. Match the exact reference numbers from finalized documents.  
**English objective:** dates, reference matching, chronology.  
**Limit:** the processing date does not prove Evelyn's location.

## Q04 — Who Does “They” Mean?

**Sources:** F04, F05  
**Task:** Resolve pronoun/reference using context and cross-document evidence.  
**Answer logic:** Accept an answer only where the text supports the referent; do not force a choice based only on nearest noun. Exact intended quote must be fixed in the final letter scripts.  
**English objective:** reference, cohesion, contextual interpretation.  
**Limit:** correct reference is not proof of culpability.

## Q05 — Restore the Missing Paragraph

**Sources:** F05, D02, D05  
**Task:** Select the missing paragraph in Beatrice's damaged letter.  
**Answer logic:** The correct paragraph must be coherent with adjacent lines and consistent with differences between original and official records. Distractors should each fail for a clear reason (contradiction, poor coherence, or unsupported claim).  
**English objective:** coherence, cohesion, vocabulary in context.  
**Limit:** record discrepancy does not establish who altered it.

## Q06 — Three Fires, One Timeline

**Sources:** D03, D04, D05, D06, D08  
**Task:** Order the key events and classify statements as facts or theories.  
**Answer logic:** 1998 first fire and Thomas's death; the 2023 fire prompted review of the old file; the 2026 fire triggers the player's current investigation. Other precise dates come from final documents.  
**English objective:** sequencing, chronology, fact vs inference.  
**Limit:** nearby sites and recurring company references do not prove one perpetrator.

## Q07 — The Evidence Board

**Sources:** all collected evidence  
**Task:** Classify statements as confirmed fact, supported inference, or unresolved question.  
**Expected classifications:**
- Thomas opposed the project after finding discrepancies: confirmed fact (once D07 and source texts are finalized).
- Thomas died in the 1998 fire: confirmed fact.
- Ashbourne benefited from land changes: confirmed fact once D05 and transaction/land records explicitly support it.
- Some identified letters were withheld: confirmed fact if E13 is authenticated and corroborated.
- Arson is the strongest theory: supported inference.
- Evelyn may have been targeted to silence her inquiry: supported inference.
- Ashbourne ordered the fires: unresolved and not proven.
- Thomas was murdered: unresolved and not proven.
- Evelyn's ultimate fate: unresolved.
**English objective:** evaluate and qualify claims.

## Q08 — The Final Report

**Prompt:**
“What is the most plausible explanation for the three fires, Thomas Wren's death, and Evelyn's disappearance? Use at least three pieces of evidence. Distinguish confirmed facts from inferences and identify at least one question that remains unresolved.”

**Length:** 100–150 words in English.

**Assessment:** use the separate rubric in `../docs/09-learning-objectives-and-assessment.md` and `../puzzles/scoring-rubric.md`.

## Red herring — Locker 07

**Task:** Follow directions, solve a code from an explicit, fair clue, inspect a personal love letter, then assess its relevance.  
**Answer:** It is unverified personal correspondence with no established link to Evelyn, Thomas, or the fires. Return to D01/E13.  
**English objective:** giving directions, expressing an evidence-based opinion.  
**Production dependencies:** final code clue, code, locker text, and letter writer identity are still TBD. The love-letter author must not be mistaken for Mayor Thomas Wren.

## Progressive hint template

1. **Observation:** direct attention to a feature or paragraph.
2. **Connection:** suggest another document worth comparing.
3. **Reasoning:** explain the logical operation required without stating the answer outright.

## Puzzle QA checklist

- [ ] Every correct answer has an explicit supporting clue.
- [ ] Every distractor is wrong for a clear reason.
- [ ] Final reference numbers are identical across every source.
- [ ] No puzzle assumes Evelyn's final fate is known.
- [ ] No puzzle declares Ashbourne guilty from motive alone.
- [ ] No puzzle turns “suspicious” into “proven arson.”
- [ ] Hints do not accidentally reveal answers to other puzzles.
