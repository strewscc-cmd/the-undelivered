# 01 — Game Design

## High concept

**Title:** THE UNDELIVERED  
**Genre:** Literary mystery / detective investigation / interactive narrative  
**Setting:** Bellwether, a fictional town  
**Investigation present:** 2026  
**Player role:** Investigative journalist  
**Learners:** Advanced English, target CEFR B2  
**Format:** Immersive website styled as a game app  
**Implementation status:** No gameplay code yet

## Player promise

The player investigates a box of undelivered letters discovered while old archives are being reviewed after a third fire in the Bellwether area. The letters are connected to Evelyn Wren, a journalism student who disappeared in 1998 while looking into documents left by her father, Mayor Thomas Wren.

The player must connect personal correspondence, postal records, land documents, fire reports, and records related to Ashbourne Land & Development without treating suspicion as proof.

## Central questions

1. What was Evelyn trying to uncover?
2. Why were some letters not delivered normally?
3. What can be established about Thomas Wren's death in the 1998 fire?
4. Why does Ashbourne appear in land documents connected to the affected area?
5. What do the 1998, 2023, and 2026 fires have in common—and what remains unproven?
6. What happened to Evelyn?

## Core loop

1. Enter a location or open an evidence source.
2. Inspect and read a document, letter, map, or witness record.
3. Mark details and save evidence to the inventory.
4. Compare references, dates, and statements.
5. Solve an English-language or evidence-reasoning puzzle.
6. Record an opinion with a reason in the Investigator's Journal.
7. Connect evidence on the Evidence Board.
8. Write a 100–150 word investigative report.

## Main chapters

- Prologue — The Third Fire (2026)
- Chapter I — The Box
- Chapter II — The Missing Daughter
- Chapter III — The Land Records
- Chapter IV — The Repeated Pattern
- Final Chapter — The Undelivered

The main branches should be explorable in a flexible order where possible. The narrative sequence above is a recommended route, not a requirement that every player discover everything in one path.

## Design pillars

- **Evidence before accusation:** every theory must be grounded in documents.
- **A human emotional core:** Evelyn's investigation starts with her father's death.
- **Connected puzzles:** information learned early should matter later.
- **Fair ambiguity:** unresolved questions are intentional, but puzzles themselves must be logically solvable.
- **English as an investigative tool:** reading, inference, directions, expressing opinions, and writing should naturally help solve the mystery.
- **Red herrings with a purpose:** false leads teach evidence evaluation rather than wasting the player's time.

## Scoring

Keep two scores separate.

### Investigation Score
- Puzzle accuracy: 40%
- Evidence connections: 35%
- Independent investigation / hint use: 25%

### English Proficiency Score
- Use of textual evidence: 30%
- Inference and reasoning: 30%
- Coherence and organization: 20%
- Language clarity: 20%

## Final task

Prompt:

> What is the most plausible explanation for the three fires, Thomas Wren's death, and Evelyn's disappearance? Use at least three pieces of evidence. Distinguish confirmed facts from inferences and identify at least one unresolved question.

Target length: 100–150 words in English.

## Boundaries

This design document does not authorize gameplay implementation yet. Final letter texts, official document texts, exact clue wording, reference numbers, and Locker 07 code still require continuity review.
